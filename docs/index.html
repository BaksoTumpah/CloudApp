Cloud

One gallery for all your Google Drive accounts. / Semua akun Drive-mu, satu galeri.

What is Cloud?

Cloud is an Android app that combines the storage of several Google Drive accounts into a single, unified gallery. You sign in to Cloud, connect one or more Google accounts, and browse, upload, organise, search and play your files from one place.

Features

  • Connect multiple Google Drive accounts and see them as one library
  • Upload, move, rename, search, and delete files and folders (deleted files go to the Drive trash)
  • Built-in video player with seeking, resume from last position, subtitles (.srt / .vtt) and a mini player
  • Select many files at once; save files for offline use on your device

How it uses your Google data

Cloud requests only the drive.file scope. It can see and manage only the files that were uploaded through Cloud, never the rest of your Drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.
