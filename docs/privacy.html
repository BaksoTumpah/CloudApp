Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Cloud app ("Cloud", "the app", "we") handles information. Cloud is developed by BaksoTumpah.

1. Information we handle

  • Cloud account: the username and password you choose, handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Google account connection: when you connect a Google account, Cloud receives an OAuth token from Google and your account's email address, used to label that account inside the app.
  • Google Drive data: file and folder names, sizes, types, thumbnails and content, limited to files that were uploaded through Cloud (drive.file scope).
  • App data: your folder organisation, the list of connected accounts, and the playback position of videos, so you can resume where you left off.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to provide the features of the app: showing your files from all connected accounts in one gallery, uploading and managing them, playing videos, and syncing your settings. We do not use it for advertising and we do not build profiles about you.

3. Where it is stored

  • Account data, refresh tokens of connected Google accounts and app data are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Security rules allow only the signed-in owner to read or write their own data.
  • Files you save for offline use are stored locally on your device in the app's storage and can be removed at any time from the app or by clearing app data.
  • File contents stay in your Google Drive. Cloud does not copy them to our servers.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information or Google user data with third parties. Data is processed only by Google services needed to run the app (Google Drive API and Firebase).

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Cloud's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while you use the app. You can disconnect a Google account inside the app, and you can revoke Cloud's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your Cloud account and all data stored in Firebase, open an issue at the contact link below and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by Firebase security rules. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your password private.

8. Children

Cloud is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this page, with the date above.

10. Contact

github.com/BaksoTumpah/CloudApp/issues
